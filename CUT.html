01. Institutional Operationalization Framework

02. National Herd Restoration & Livestock Innovation

Funded through the Ministry of Higher and Tertiary Education's Research, Development and Commercialisation Fund (RDCIF), the Germplasm Laboratory processes genetically superior bull semen to compress inter-calving periods and elevate national calving success. Concurrently, the CUT Agro Plant manufactures 100% urea-free supplement licks to eliminate feed toxicity risks.

03. Circular Bioeconomy & Black Soldier Fly Larviculture

To mitigate feed-cost inflation in non-ruminant livestock, the park operates a Black Soldier Fly (Hermetia illucens) bioconversion unit. Organic waste collected from university commercial hotels is transformed into high-protein larval meal and high-grade organic fertilizer.

04. Precision IoT Telemetry & Soil Hydrology

Deployed across 10-hectare maize and soybean seed blocks, a 48-node wireless sensor network (WSN) transmits real-time volumetric water content (VWC %), air temperatures, and precipitation data via LoRaWAN. The system actively tracks soil moisture against a Management Allowed Depletion (MAD) threshold of 20%.

120-Day Soil Moisture Dynamic Telemetry vs MAD Threshold

Field Capacity: 35.0% | MAD Limit: 20.0%

Continuous VWC telemetry demonstrates moisture decay punctuated by rainfall recharges. Drops below 20% MAD trigger automated irrigation alerts.

06. Extension Impact & Strategic Roadmap (2025–2030)

Beyond institutional revenue, the park drives rural climate adaptation across 230 Farmer Field Schools (FFS) utilizing Participatory Integrated Climate Services for Agriculture (PICSA) methodologies.

Phase 1 (2025–2026)

Feed Registration & Scaling

Complete formal product registration for BSF larval feeds under the Farm Feeds Act. Expand municipal waste collection to reach 3 tonnes per cycle output.

Phase 2 (2027–2028)

Provincial Hub Expansion

Establish university retail and extension hubs in rural districts to broaden smallholder access to urea-free mineral blocks and AI breeding services.

Phase 3 (2029–2030)

Cross-Border Agro-Industrial Park

Deepen integration with the Zambia–Zimbabwe CAIP framework. Implement a unified "One Health" matrix uniting animal biosecurity and crop production.
